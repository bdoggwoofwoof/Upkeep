# Upkeep design notes

**Purpose**: take the mental load out of running a house: every chore and maintenance job, on the right schedule, split fairly.
**People**: a household. One adult sets it up, other adults join with the invite link, kids use Kid view on a shared device.
**When**: kitchen in the morning, couch at night for the closing shift, garage or yard with dirty hands. Visits are 5–30 seconds; the shift about 2 minutes.
**Must be perfect**: seeing what's due and checking it off in one tap. Records, expenses and settings can be clunky.

**Feel**: calm, capable, warm. Never naggy, childish or corporate. A well-kept utility closet or the clipboard by the back door.
**Playfulness**: only in Kid view, stars and rewards, and finishing the closing shift.
**Look**: its own look with native patterns (bottom tabs, sheets).

**Color**: light and dark, following the phone. Door green accent (#2D5D4C light, #86C5AB dark), amber for soon, rust for late. No neon, no purple, no gradients. The interface stays neutral; people's colors and photos add color.
**Type**: Young Serif for headings, Instrument Sans for text, JetBrains Mono for dates and numbers. Confident but not huge headings.
**Layout**: medium-density lists, big cards in Kid view, bottom tab bar (Today, Shift, House, Family, More). First screen is Today.
**Motion**: subtle. Small delight on finishing the shift and kids earning stars. Respect reduced motion.
**Words**: plain and short with some warmth. Use "Done means", "Up for grabs", "Away mode". Avoid "failed", "lazy", guilt.
**Icons**: with labels.
**Practical**: iPhone first, Android, iPad for Kid view; larger text; never color alone for status; works offline.
**App family**: shared fonts, spacing and icon style across Newt's apps with a different accent each. Icon: rounded square, solid accent, one line icon (house with a check).
